Why do we harm ourselves and others?

My research in psychological science centers on this existential question about human nature. I investigate the cognitive, affective, and motivational mechanisms underlying destructive behaviors directed toward the self, such as suicide and addiction, as well as toward others, such as stigmatization, dehumanization, and violence.

Research areas

Scientific questions, human implications.

Exploring how psychological processes shape thought, behavior, and mental health.

My work addresses social cognition, aggression, implicit processes, addiction, and the psychology of suicide. It combines experimental methods, clinical data, and, more recently, artificial intelligence.
